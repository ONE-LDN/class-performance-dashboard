Time correction — what moved

WodBoard exports Start time in UTC. During British Summer Time every class therefore reads an hour early. The fix converts UTC → Europe/London at ingestion, which is a no-op in winter and +1 hour in summer. Confirmed three ways: the 06:15 HYROX reading 05:15, Built reading 08:30 against your stated 09:30, and Gym Time spanning 05:00–19:00 where opening hours are 06:00–20:00.
